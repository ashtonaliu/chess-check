    How it works

    1. Your games come straight from the Chess.com public API.
    2. Stockfish scores every position, and every blunder is double-checked with a deeper search.
    3. Tactics such as forks and discovered attacks are recognised on the board and compared with your opponents' rates.
    4. Openings are judged against what your rating predicts, with small samples treated cautiously.
    5. An AI coach writes the report, and a checker verifies every claim against the data.
    Data: Chess.com public API. Engine: Stockfish.js (GPL-3.0, served unmodified). Puzzles: Lichess. Source code.
