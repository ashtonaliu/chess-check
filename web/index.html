Weakness Finder

Finds where you actually lose points in your Chess.com games. Results are adjusted for opponent rating and sample size, checked with Stockfish, and explained by a coach whose every claim is verified.

Openings, adjusted for rating

Dot: your score minus the Elo-expected score, shrunk toward your average when the sample is small. Bar: 90% interval. Hollow dot: the raw, unshrunk number.

An agent with SQL, retrieval and Stockfish tools writes the report. A checker then verifies every number, move and link against the facts and tool results it was given.

Data: Chess.com public API. Engine: Stockfish.js (GPL-3.0, served unmodified). Puzzles: Lichess.
